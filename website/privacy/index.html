Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026
Publisher: DataLund
Website: https://datalund.no/

DataLund Power BI visuals (including DataLund Gantt) run inside the Power BI sandbox. They do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

What the visual processes

  • Only the fields you bind in a Power BI report (for example task names, dates, progress, groups, resources, and tooltip fields).
  • All rendering happens locally in Power BI Desktop or the Power BI service.
  • The visual does not call external HTTP/S or WebSocket endpoints.
  • The visual does not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.

Permissions

DataLund Gantt declares no network or storage privileges in its Power BI capabilities. That means it cannot access the internet from inside the visual sandbox.

This website

datalund.no is a product and support site. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to respond to your request. We do not sell contact information.

If the site uses standard hosting logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp), those logs are used only for security and reliability, not for advertising profiles.

Children

Our visuals and site are intended for business and professional use, not for children under 16.
