Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Publisher: Datalund · datalund.no

Datalund Power BI visuals (including DataLund Gantt) run inside the Power BI sandbox. They do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

What the visual processes

  • Only the fields you bind in a Power BI report (task names, dates, progress, groups, resources, tooltip fields).
  • All rendering happens locally in Power BI Desktop or the Power BI service.
  • The visual does not call external HTTP/S or WebSocket endpoints.
  • The visual does not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.

Permissions

DataLund Gantt declares no network or storage privileges in its Power BI capabilities, so it cannot access the internet from inside the visual sandbox.

This website

datalund.no is a product and support site. Email you send us is used only to respond. We do not sell contact information. Standard hosting logs (IP, user agent, timestamp), if present, are used for security and reliability only.
